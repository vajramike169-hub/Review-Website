DealAegis Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Developer: VajraHowly • Bundle ID: com.vajrahowly.dealaegis • Contact: howly7777777@gmail.com
Privacy Guarantee: DealAegis collects zero personal data, zero usage analytics, zero tracking identifiers, and never uploads confidential documents to any cloud server.

1. 100% On-Device Processing (Air-Gapped Architecture)

All PDF text extraction (PDFKit), optical character recognition (Apple Vision OCR), semantic clause inspection, cross-page financial consistency audits, and v1/v2 redline comparisons execute strictly locally on your iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad, or Mac. You can enable Airplane Mode and turn off Wi-Fi without affecting any core audit functionality.

2. Local Hardware-Encrypted Storage

Custom contracts and deal rooms you choose to save are encrypted locally using AES-256-GCM with keys stored inside Apple’s Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. DealAegis operates no remote servers, cloud storage buckets, or user account databases.

3. Camera & Photo Library Access (User-Initiated Only)

DealAegis requests Camera or Photo Library permission solely when you explicitly choose to scan paper pages or import an image of a contract clause. Images are processed in local device memory for OCR and are never transmitted outside your device.

4. In-App Subscriptions & Apple Standard EULA

Optional auto-renewable subscriptions (DealAegis Pro Monthly and DealAegis Pro Annual) are processed strictly by Apple’s official StoreKit 2 framework. DealAegis verifies active entitlements locally via Apple-signed StoreKit transactions and does not collect payment details. Use of DealAegis is governed by the Apple Standard EULA.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

Because all data remains exclusively on your hardware, you may permanently delete any custom deal project inside DealAegis or erase all local app files by uninstalling the app at any time.

For support, privacy verification, or compliance questions, email howly7777777@gmail.com or visit the DealAegis Support Home.
